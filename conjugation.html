French grammar / Fransuz tili grammatikasi

1. Présent

English: Used for actions happening now, habits and general facts.

O‘zbekcha: Hozir sodir bo‘layotgan ishlar, odatlar va umumiy haqiqatlar uchun ishlatiladi.

Example: Je travaille. – I work. – Men ishlayman.

2. Passé composé

English: Describes a completed action in the past. It uses avoir or être + past participle.

O‘zbekcha: O‘tmishda tugallangan ish-harakat. Avoir yoki être yordamchi fe’li va participe passé bilan yasaladi.

Example: J'ai travaillé. – I worked. – Men ishladim.

3. Imparfait

English: Used for habits, descriptions and ongoing situations in the past.

O‘zbekcha: O‘tmishdagi odatlar, tasvirlar va davom etgan holatlar uchun ishlatiladi.

Example: Je travaillais. – I used to work. – Men ishlardim.

4. Plus-que-parfait

English: An action completed before another past action.

O‘zbekcha: O‘tmishdagi boshqa bir ish-harakatdan oldin tugagan ish.

Example: J'avais travaillé. – I had worked. – Men ishlagan edim.

5. Futur simple

English: Used for future actions and predictions.

O‘zbekcha: Kelajakdagi ish-harakatlar va taxminlar uchun ishlatiladi.

Example: Je travaillerai. – I will work. – Men ishlayman.

6. Futur antérieur

English: An action that will be completed before a future moment.

O‘zbekcha: Kelajakdagi ma'lum bir vaqtgacha tugallangan bo‘ladigan ish.

7. Conditionnel présent

English: Used for polite requests, possibilities and hypothetical situations.

O‘zbekcha: Muloyim iltimos, ehtimol va shartli vaziyatlarda ishlatiladi.

8. Conditionnel passé

English: Describes what would have happened.

O‘zbekcha: O‘tmishda sodir bo‘lishi mumkin bo‘lgan, ammo sodir bo‘lmagan ishni bildiradi.

9. Subjonctif présent

English: Often follows expressions of necessity, emotion, doubt or desire.

O‘zbekcha: Zarurat, his-tuyg‘u, shubha yoki istakni bildiruvchi iboralardan keyin ishlatiladi.

10. Subjonctif passé

English: Expresses a completed action in a subjunctive context.

O‘zbekcha: Subjonctif talab qilinadigan gapda tugallangan ish-harakatni bildiradi.
